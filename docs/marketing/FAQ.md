# FAQ

## Is Soleaux an AI coding agent?

No. Soleaux supplies repository intelligence and governed lifecycle services to existing agents and clients.

## Is it an agent operating system?

No. The product definition is unified repository intelligence.

## Why only 12 tools?

Large root catalogs consume context and recreate MCP sprawl. Soleaux keeps the public profile fixed and loads depth through registry resources, gateway namespaces, CLI/service operations, and one-for-one substitutions.

## What does `context.compile` return?

A bounded `soleaux.context/v2` packet with source evidence, owners, consumers, constraints, conflicts, validation routes, supporting facts, resources, gaps, provenance, trust, redaction, continuations, and budget measurements.

## Does Soleaux replace Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, OpenCode, or Cursor?

No. It is attached to or integrated with those clients.

## Does Soleaux write to their internal databases?

No. Vendor-native identifiers are mappings; the Soleaux daemon owns its canonical state.

## What has been proven?

Phases 0, 1, 2, and 4 are closed. Evidence covers native compilation and strict gates, the exact 12-tool profile, Context Packet V2, gateway/catalog/provisioning/governance, transactional editing and provisioning, fresh reads, comprehensive redaction, canonical state and recovery, encrypted artifacts and capability policy, stable CLI, per-user service, typed IPC, and a reproducible unsigned development alpha exercised through install, restart, doctor, backup, export, repair, restore, and uninstall.

## Is the unsigned alpha a signed release?

No. It is a reproducible, independently verified development artifact. Signing, notarization, stores, and general availability remain later gates.

## What remains?

Phase 5 live client adapters, canonical lifecycle and materializers, intelligence depth, SDKs, editor integration, and design partners; Phase 6 desktop/mobile/remote/installers; Phase 7 assurance; Phase 8 signed staged release. The deferred Phase 3 three-arm proof also remains mandatory before quantified efficacy claims.
